# Reused full-project assets

The photographs and driving video below are copied from the full MotorHub project. Stock photographs illustrate fictional listings; they are not evidence of the condition of an actual seller vehicle.

## Hero video

The homepage uses real driving footage: **Sports Car on the Road** by **Ojyrai Films**, available on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/video/sports-car-on-the-road-7727415/), under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Retrieved 9 November 2026. The license permits use and modification on websites.

`frontend/public/media/motorhub-drive.mp4` is a 14-second, silent 720p excerpt (source seconds 6–20). The source is actual footage of a Lamborghini driving through a city, not a photo montage. `motorhub-drive-poster.jpg` is a frame from the same footage. The original 42-second download is kept only in scratch work and is not distributed.
